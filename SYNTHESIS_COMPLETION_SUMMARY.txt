LIMITATIONS ENCOUNTERED
================================================================================

1. MEMORY CONSTRAINTS (Hard Limit)
   - Full state enumeration of FT10 exceeds 10 GB at depth 10
   - Problem: O(exp^n) state storage requirement
   - Solution needed: Out-of-core algorithms or sampling

2. OSCILLATOR COMPLEXITY (Computational Cost)
   - N=560 oscillators per benchmark expensive for very deep searches
   - Problem: O(N²) coupling calculation
   - Solution: Hierarchical oscillator groups or reduced dynamics

3. SPEEDUP DIMINISHES DEEPER (Effectiveness Limit)
   - Speedup peaks at moderate depths, then flattens
   - Problem: Tree irregularity and depth-dependent branching
   - Solution: Dynamic coupling adjustment or multi-scale approach

4. RANDOM SEARCH LIMITS PRUNING BENEFIT (Algorithm Dependency)
   - Random selection wastes pruning decisions
   - Problem: Suboptimal branching choices
   - Solution: Integrate with informed heuristics (A*, branch-and-cut)
